# The Forgotten Garden

### Quietbind — Story #6

**Genre:** Cozy Romance • Family Mystery • Drama • Slice of Life

---

## Overview

At the edge of the quiet town of Evergrove lies **Rosewood Estate**,
abandoned for nearly thirty years. Once famous for its breathtaking
botanical garden, it is now overgrown with vines, broken statues, and
locked greenhouses.

After her grandmother's passing, **Lily Bennett** unexpectedly inherits
the estate. The will contains only one request:

> **"Make the garden bloom one last time."**

As Lily restores each section of the garden, she uncovers hidden
letters, forgotten journals, and old photographs buried beneath the
flowers. The deeper she digs, the clearer it becomes: her grandmother
wasn't simply tending flowers. She was protecting a family secret
powerful enough to destroy generations of lives.

---

## Main Characters

**Lily Bennett** (23) — Landscape architecture graduate. Creative,
optimistic, and stubborn. Returns home believing she's only restoring a
garden. Instead, she begins restoring broken lives.

**Rowan Hayes** (26) — Botanist. Quiet, patient. Has cared for the
abandoned greenhouse for years without telling anyone. Knows every
flower by name.

**Elliot Carter** (24) — Local carpenter. Warm, dependable, always
smiling. Helps Lily repair the estate while hiding feelings he's carried
since childhood.

**Sebastian Vale** (27) — Historian, recently arrived in Evergrove to
research the Bennett family. His investigation uncovers long-buried
scandals.

**Eleanor Bennett** — Lily's late grandmother. Her journals become the
heart of the mystery — every restored flowerbed reveals another page of
her story.

---

## Core Mystery

Why did Eleanor abandon the garden she loved most? Who sealed the
western greenhouse? What happened to the Bennett family forty years
ago? And why does someone keep destroying Lily's progress each night?

---

## Story Structure (20 Chapters)

### Act I — Seeds of the Past (Ch. 1–5)
1. Lily arrives at Rosewood Estate.
2. The first flower blooms — a hidden key is discovered beneath it.
3. The western greenhouse remains locked.
4. A mysterious figure is seen walking through the garden at night.
5. The first journal is found beneath an old rose tree.

### Act II — Blooming Secrets (Ch. 6–10)
Every restored section of the estate uncovers another clue. Old family
feuds emerge, and the player slowly rebuilds both the garden and the
town's trust. Affection with the love interests grows naturally through
shared work.

### Act III — Hidden Roots (Ch. 11–15)
Route selection. Each romance reveals different pieces of Eleanor's
past. The western greenhouse is finally opened — inside waits a
forgotten memorial, and the truth about the Bennett family.

### Act IV — The Final Bloom (Ch. 16–18)
Lily discovers Eleanor sacrificed her own happiness to protect someone
she loved. The family's greatest secret is finally revealed, and the
person sabotaging the garden is confronted.

### Act V — Spring Returns (Ch. 19–20)
The estate hosts its first Flower Festival in decades. The player's
choices determine the future of both the garden and Lily's heart.

---

## Romance Routes

**Rowan Route** — A gentle slow-burn romance built through restoring
nature together. His knowledge of plants hides clues left behind by
Eleanor.

**Elliot Route** — Friends-to-lovers. Working side by side rebuilding
the estate strengthens their bond. A heartfelt route focused on healing
and home.

**Sebastian Route** — A mystery-focused romance. History, journals, and
forgotten family records slowly reveal the truth.

---

## Endings

**True Ending** — The Bennett family's secret is revealed with
compassion instead of anger. Rosewood Estate becomes a public botanical
garden. Lily and her chosen partner begin a new chapter together.

**Good Ending** — The garden is restored, but some mysteries remain
unanswered. Life moves forward with hope.

**Bittersweet Ending** — Lily discovers the full truth but chooses to
keep Eleanor's final promise, leaving part of the past buried forever.

**Bad Ending** — The estate is sold to developers. The garden is
demolished before its story can be told.

**Secret Ending** — A hidden flower blooms only once every fifty years.
Inside its petals is a map leading to another forgotten place, teasing
the next Quietbind adventure. *(Fourth cross-story hook — Midnight Café,
Crimson Masquerade, Echoes of Tomorrow, and now this one all point at
something bigger. Genuinely time to decide if that's a real meta-plot.)*

**Tragic Ending** — To save the estate from destruction, Lily sacrifices
her inheritance and walks away. The garden blooms again, but she never
returns.

## Themes

Healing across generations • Family legacy • Forgiveness • Finding home
• Nature and renewal • Love that grows with time

## Tone

Warm spring mornings, birdsong, overgrown paths, cozy cottages,
handwritten journals, flower festivals, and quiet romance. This story
emphasizes emotional healing, rebuilding relationships, and uncovering
family history while maintaining a relaxing, hopeful atmosphere that
deliberately contrasts with the darker mysteries found in other
Quietbind titles.
