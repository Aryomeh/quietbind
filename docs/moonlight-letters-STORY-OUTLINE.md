# Moonlight Letters

### Quietbind — Story #2

**Genre:** Romance • Mystery • Drama • Slice of Life

---

## Overview

Every full moon, anonymous handwritten letters appear inside the town's
abandoned red mailbox. Each letter predicts something that will happen the
following day — and every prediction comes true.

At first they're harmless:

> "Don't forget your umbrella."
> "Someone will return your lost necklace."
> "The old clock will stop at exactly 4:17."

Then one letter changes everything:

> **"Do not fall in love with him."**

The heroine ignores the warning. That single choice begins unraveling a
twenty-year-old mystery involving a disappeared girl, a forgotten train
accident, and someone who has been writing letters from the shadows for
years.

---

## Main Characters

**Emma Hart** (22) — A quiet illustrator who returns to her hometown after
inheriting her grandmother's tiny stationery shop. Loves sketching flowers
and restoring old journals.

**Adrian Cross** (24) — Local librarian. Reserved, observant, knows nearly
every forgotten story in town. His mother disappeared years ago.

**Noah Blake** (25) — Journalist. Funny, confident, always chasing the next
headline. Believes the letters are connected to an unsolved disappearance.

**Ethan Vale** (23) — Childhood friend, police officer. Protective, hides
feelings he's had since they were children.

**Eleanor Ashford** — Emma's late grandmother. Left behind dozens of
journals... and one final sealed letter.

**The Letter Writer** — Unknown. No one has ever seen them. Every letter is
written in identical handwriting.

## Chapter Structure (20 Chapters)

### Act I — The Letters Begin (Ch. 1–5)

**1. Home Again** — Emma inherits Hart Stationery after her grandmother's
death. Cleaning outside, she finds an old red mailbox with one handwritten
letter inside: *"Welcome home."*

**2. The Prediction** — The letter predicts tomorrow's events. Every
prediction comes true. Coincidence? Emma isn't sure.

**3. The Librarian** — Emma meets Adrian, who quietly mentions: *"You're not
the first person to receive those letters."*

**4. Midnight Delivery** — Another full moon, another letter. No one sees
who delivers it — security cameras fail.

**5. A Forgotten Name** — Emma finds newspaper clippings about a girl who
disappeared twenty years ago. One article is torn in half; only one name
remains — "Luna."

### Act II — Following the Clues (Ch. 6–10)

**6. The Journalist** — Noah convinces Emma to investigate. They visit the
abandoned train tracks together.

**7. The Station Master** — An elderly man remembers strange letters
arriving decades ago, but refuses to say more.

**8. The Hidden Journal** — Behind a loose floorboard in the stationery
shop, Emma finds Eleanor's diary. Every page mentions "the letters."

**9. Full Moon Festival** — During the town festival, another prediction
arrives: someone will disappear before sunrise. Everyone laughs — then
someone actually vanishes.

**10. Three Suspects** — Evidence points toward Adrian, Noah, and Ethan.
Each has been hiding something.

### Act III — Love and Lies (Ch. 11–15)

**11. Route Lock** — Player chooses whose trust to follow. Affection
totals determine the romance route from here.

**12. Broken Promises** — The chosen love interest reveals their biggest
secret.

**13. The Old Tunnel** — Emma discovers a hidden railway tunnel containing
hundreds of unopened letters.

**14. Twenty Years Ago** — Flashbacks reveal the train accident wasn't an
accident — someone covered it up.

**15. The Final Letter** — Emma receives the only letter ever addressed to
her by name: *"Emma... you're almost out of time."*

### Act IV — Truth (Ch. 16–18)

**16. The Missing Girl** — "Luna" never disappeared. Someone erased her
existence.

**17. The Letter Writer** — Identity revealed. The letters were never
supernatural — they were written by someone protecting the truth for two
decades.

**18. The Last Prediction** — One final letter predicts the ending. The
player decides whether to follow it, or destroy it forever.

### Act V — Endings (Ch. 19–20)

**19.** Route-dependent major confrontation; the mystery resolves.

**20.** One of six endings plays, based on route and final affection.

## Romance Routes

**Adrian Route** — Quiet romance, library dates, a shared love of books;
truth hidden inside forgotten journals.
*Endings:* True Love / Bittersweet / Tragic

**Noah Route** — Adventure, investigation, road trips; exposing the
conspiracy together.
*Endings:* Happy / Sacrifice / Bad Ending

**Ethan Route** — Childhood friends, protective romance, police
investigation; choosing duty or love.
*Endings:* Marriage / Goodbye / Heartbreak
